# PCB completion audit

This is the blocking work list for a fabrication release. The release gate must
not be weakened to clear an item.

## Blocking

- **Reed/magnet stack unproven.** The selected KSK-1A66-1015 lies horizontally
  below a vertical magnet. One-square testing at the final CAD spacing is still
  mandatory before buying the large board. Record the evidence in `prototype/`;
  the release gate intentionally refuses to emit Gerbers until it exists.

## Automated gates currently passing

- Copper routing is complete: KiCad reports zero geometry violations and zero
  unconnected items across reed inputs, LED links, rails, buses, interrupts,
  and controls.
- The native schematic exists, ERC passes, and schematic/PCB parity reports zero
  errors.
- Every placed item resolves to an exact manufacturer part number, typed
  component pin model, and package-compatible footprint.

## Product and mechanical review

- Confirm availability and datasheets for every MPN in
  `hardware/shared/components.py`, especially SK9822-EC20, the Waveshare 10444
  display, and RA11131100 rocker switch.
- Select exact M3 thread-forming screws for the twenty new PCB mounting holes and
  add them to the approved-parts catalog and BOM.
- Verify the generated custom footprints against dimensioned manufacturer
  drawings. Courtyards now use real body envelopes and KiCad rejects missing or
  malformed courtyards.
- Add KiCad 3D models for assembly-height and connector-access review. Current 3D
  renders show board, pads, drills, planes, and copper but not component bodies.
- Obtain the fabricator's actual eight-layer stack-up and confirm dielectric,
  finished thickness, copper weight, via aspect ratio, and plane clearances.

## Electrical review

- Review worst-case LED current, plane voltage drop, fuse clearing behavior, TVS
  clamp voltage, and Pi brown-out margin.
- Confirm the brightness cap is enforced by the host rather than left to an
  application convention.
- Confirm all 27 explicit no-connect pins against the final native schematic and
  each device datasheet.
- Add test points or a bed-of-nails plan for every rail, bus, interrupt, and LED
  chain endpoint required during bring-up.

## Release evidence

A release requires all of the following generated by `./tools/pcb`:

1. Zero KiCad DRC violations and zero unconnected items.
2. Zero DRC exclusions and zero ignored rule severities.
3. Zero schematic parity errors.
4. Exact-MPN BOM and matching footprints.
5. Board-fitted SVGs and orthographic/perspective 3D renders.
6. Gerber and drill output created only after the preceding gates pass.
